Amigurumi math

The pattern says "insert eyes between rounds 12 and 13" and leaves the size to you; the stuffing bag never says how many toys it makes. Amigurumi math sizes the safety eyes from the head you actually crocheted, weighs the fiberfill from the toy's dimensions, and counts the bags before you buy. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Eye sizing - head circumference becomes an eye size and a placement gap.
  • Stuffing weight - toy dimensions become the fiberfill to plan on.
  • Bag count - toys and per-toy grams become the bags to buy.

Labeled norms

Eyes: about 1/6 of the head diameter, placed about half a diameter apart. Stuffing: about 12 g per liter, toy volume estimated as an ellipsoid. Labeled craft ratios, not rules - your pattern and your tension win. Safety eyes are a choking hazard for children under three - embroider instead.
